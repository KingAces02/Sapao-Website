The barangay

About Barangay Sapao

A coastal community shaped by war history, faith, fishing, and the quiet work of the people who keep it running.

Origins

A coastline with a wartime past

Barangay Sapao is a coastal community in the municipality of Guiuan, Eastern Samar, Philippines. It played a significant role during World War II as part of the expansive U.S. Naval Base in Guiuan, which served as a strategic point for Allied Forces in the Pacific. The barangay is home to the Parish of the Holy Cross, a Roman Catholic parish established as a quasi-parish in 1999 and formally canonically erected in 2004. The parish celebrates its feast day every May 7 in honor of the Holy Cross.

Sapao is also known for its natural attractions, including Sapao Beach, with its white sands and diverse shells offering scenic views of the Pacific Ocean. The barangay hosts essential community facilities such as the Barangay Hall, Day Care Center, and Basketball Court. Additionally, Sapao is the location of a PAGASA weather station equipped with a Doppler radar system, contributing to regional weather monitoring and forecasting.

Governance

Community development

Barangay Sapao continues to thrive through the dedication and hard work of its local officials. Their responsibilities are wide-ranging — maintaining peace, implementing community development projects, and improving the quality of life for residents, while enforcing rules and regulations that promote order and growth. One project currently underway is the "I Love You Sapao" initiative, which aims to foster unity and pride within the barangay, though it remains ongoing as officials prepare for the much-anticipated annual fiesta.

Fishing remains the primary source of livelihood for many residents, given the barangay's proximity to the sea. Understanding the needs of their constituents, the barangay officials actively support working individuals and low-income families by facilitating programs from the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and initiating projects that benefit the community's most vulnerable members. The fiesta of Sta. Cruz is a major event here, celebrated with a vesper night and a solemn mass, often officiated by a bishop — festivities that reflect the community's devotion and strengthen its cultural ties. Barangay Sapao is home to two sitios, San Roque and Dumrog, each with its own unique celebrations and traditions.

This project

About the team

The 2nd year students of Eastern Samar State University of Guiuan aimed to help the Barangay of Sapao grow its tourism by building a website about the history and tourist spots in the barangay. This project supports a shared goal of using technology to strengthen communities, promote tourism, and build local pride in the digital age.
